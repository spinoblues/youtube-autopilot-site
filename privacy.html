Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

YouTube Autopilot is a private creator utility operated by the site owner. Questions: jayceeeeh@gmail.com.

Google data and permissions

The application requests YouTube upload authorization to upload videos to the YouTube channel selected by the consenting account owner. Google may provide account and channel information necessary to authenticate and perform that upload. We do not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are intended to be held in restricted automation secrets, not published on this website. Video assets and processing logs may be held by the operator's automation services and GitHub. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

Data is transmitted to Google/YouTube to perform authorized uploads and to infrastructure providers used to operate the automation. It is not sold to data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Automation credentials and logs are retained as needed for the workflow until removed by the operator. To request deletion or ask about retained data, email the contact address above. Revoking access in Google Account connections prevents further authorized API access; the operator must also delete stored credentials where applicable.

Google API Services

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the automation changes. Review it periodically.
