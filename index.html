CREATOR TOOL

YouTube Autopilot

A private automation utility for preparing and publishing original videos to a YouTube channel authorized by its owner.

How it works

The operator creates or selects videos, checks technical quality, and uses Google's OAuth authorization to upload videos through the YouTube API. This site describes the tool; it does not itself collect Google account sign-ins.

Account control

Only the channel owner grants permission. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account third-party connections.
